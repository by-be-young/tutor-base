# 第一部分：谓语 / 非谓语的基本判断与选择

**题组：非谓语的被动关系 vs 谓语的被动语态**

【题干1】The famous actor, together with his wife and children, ______ (award) the Best Actor prize last night.

【题干2】The famous actor showed up with his wife and children, who ______ (award) the Best Actor prize last night.

【题干3】The famous actor showed up with his wife and children, ______ (award) the Best Actor prize last night.

**题组：非谓语的完成状态 vs 谓语的时态**

【题干4】The problem ______ (discuss) at the meeting just now is about global warming.

【题干5】The problem ______ (discuss) at the meeting now is about global warming.

【题干6】The problem ______ (discuss) at the meeting tomorrow is about global warming.

【题干7】The problem which ______ (discuss) at the meeting just now is about global warming.

【题干8】The problem which ______ (discuss) at the meeting now is about global warming.

【题干9】The problem which ______ (discuss) at the meeting tomorrow is about global warming.

**题组：动词在从句里 vs 动词在主句里**

【题干10】The scientist entered the lab, ______ (follow) by his assistants.

【题干11】The scientist that entered the lab ______ (follow) by his assistants.

【题干12】The scientist entered the lab, who ______ (follow) by his assistants.

**非谓语作主语**

【题干13】______ (get) along well with others is a valuable skill.

【题干14】______ (finish) this task today is our target right now.

**非谓语作宾语**

【题干15】The government has taken measures to prevent the disease from ______ (spread).

【题干16】She refused ______ (accept) the invitation to the party.

【题干17】The thief escaped ______ (catch) by the police.

**非谓语作定语**

【题干18】The girl ______ (sit) in the corner is my sister.

【题干19】The book ______ (write) by Lu Xun is popular among students.

【题干20】The ______ (frighten) children cried loudly.

【题干21】The best way ______ (learn) English is to practice every day.

**非谓语作状语**

【题干22】The boy is old enough ______ (go) to school.

【题干23】______ (absorb) in the book, he didn't notice me enter.

【题干24】The old man sat in the chair, ______ (read) a newspaper.

**非谓语作补语**

【题干25】I found him ______ (lie) on the grass, reading a book.

【题干26】The old man sat in the chair, with his eyes ______ (close).

【题干27】The boy was made ______ (stand) outside for an hour.
