# 一、重点词汇

## 1. 字母组合与音标

| 字母组合 | 例词 | 音标 | 提示 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ea | team / each / least / lead | /iː/ | 多数 ea 读长音 i |
| ea | break | /eɪ/ | 少数例外，与 great 同类，需单独记 |
| ay | always | /eɪ/ | 词尾 ay 读 eɪ |
| ai | fail | /feɪl/ | ai 与 ay 同音 |
| ph | physical | /f/ | ph 读 f，不读 p 加 h |
| or | forms | /ɔː/ | or 在重读音节常读 ɔː，对照 work 的 /ɜː/ 需分开记 |
| -s 尾音 | tips / forms / promises | /s/、/z/、/ɪz/ | 清音后读 s，浊音后读 z，-es 后读 ɪz |

## 2. 词块

| 词块 | 释义 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| team building | 团队建设 | Team building helps new members fit in quickly. |
| refer to | 指的是；指代 | “Team building” refers to activities that bring people closer. |
| be designed to do | 旨在做，为……而设计 | These games are designed to help new members know each other. |
| bring … closer | 使……更亲近 | A shared dinner brings colleagues closer. |
| come in many forms | 有多种形式 | Online courses come in many forms, including live classes. |
| including | 包括 | We tried three forms, including skill-based and social ones. |

# 二、完整原文

Have you ever heard of **team building**? It **refers to** activities and methods designed to bring team members **closer** and improve their teamwork. It comes in **many forms**, **including skill-based, social, and physical team building.**

**However**, making a team work well is **not always easy**. **One of the key challenges** is that team members fail to **trust each other**. Trust is the base of any good team, but building it **takes time and effort**.

To make your team building successful, here are some tips. **First of all**, it is important to **lead by example**. If you often **break promises**, it is hard for others to build trust within the team. **What's more**, encourage open communication. **Last but not least**, hold trust-building activities regularly.
