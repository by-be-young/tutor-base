| 字母组合 | 例词 | 音标 | 提示 |

## 2. 词块

| 词块 | 释义 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| To make … successful, here are some tips. | 要让……成功，这里有一些建议 | To make our class meeting useful, here are some tips. |
| First of all | 首先 | First of all, set a clear goal for each meeting. |
| it is important to do | 做……很重要 | It is important to listen before you speak. |
| lead by example | 以身作则 | Group leaders should lead by example. |
| break promises | 食言，不守承诺 | If you break promises, people stop trusting you. |
| What's more | 而且，此外 | What's more, we can share progress in the group chat. |
| encourage open communication | 鼓励坦诚沟通 | Teachers should encourage open communication in class. |
| Last but not least | 最后同样重要的是 | Last but not least, review your notes every week. |
| hold … regularly | 定期举办…… | We hold a reading night regularly. |

# 二、完整原文

Have you ever heard of **team building**? / It **refers to** activities and methods / designed to bring team members **closer** / and improve their teamwork. / It comes in **many forms**, / **including skill-based, social, and physical team building.**

**However** / making a team work well / is **not always easy**. / **One of the key challenges** / is that team members fail to **trust each other**. / Trust is the base of any good team, / but building it **takes time and effort**.

To make your team building successful, / here are some tips. / **First of all**, / it is important to **lead by example**. / If you often **break promises**, / it is hard for others to build trust within the team. / **What's more**, / encourage open communication. / **Last but not least**, / hold trust-building activities regularly.
