# 一、重点词汇

## 1. 字母组合与音标

| 字母组合 | 例词 | 音标 | 提示 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| th | methods | /ˈmeθədz/ | 名词、数词中的 th 多读清音 θ |
| th | other / with / within | /ˈʌðə/ /wɪð/ /wɪˈðɪn/ | 代词与介词中的 th 多读浊音 ð |
| ch | each / challenges | /iːtʃ/ /ˈtʃælɪndʒɪz/ | ch 读 tʃ，不读 k |
| tion | communication | /ˌkɒmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/ | tion 读 ʃn，全段只出现一次，务必读准 |
| ti | activities | /ækˈtɪvətiz/ | 不是 tion 的 ti 仍读 tɪ |
| oo | good | /gʊd/ | oo 也可读短音 ʊ，对照 food 的 /uː/ |
| ear | heard | /hɜːd/ | ear 在 heard 中读 ɜː，不是 ɪə |

## 2. 词块

| 词块 | 释义 | 例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| However, … | 然而；不过 | However, our group met a big problem in week two. |
| make a team work well | 让团队运转良好 | Clear rules make a team work well. |
| not always easy | 并不总是容易 | Working online is not always easy for new members. |
| One of the key challenges is that … | 关键挑战之一是…… | One of the key challenges is that members fail to share information. |
| fail to do sth | 未能做成某事 | Some members fail to finish their part on time. |
| trust each other | 互相信任 | Good teammates trust each other instead of checking every detail. |
| the base of | ……的基础 | Everyday communication is the base of a strong team. |
| take time and effort | 需要时间和努力 | Building trust takes time and effort, so start with small tasks. |

# 二、完整原文

Have you ever heard of **team building**? It **refers to** activities and methods designed to bring team members **closer** and improve their teamwork. It comes in **many forms**, **including skill-based, social, and physical team building.**

**However**, making a team work well is **not always easy**. **One of the key challenges** is that team members fail to **trust each other**. Trust is the base of any good team, but building it **takes time and effort**.

To make your team building successful, here are some tips. **First of all**, it is important to **lead by example**. If you often **break promises**, it is hard for others to build trust within the team. **What's more**, encourage open communication. **Last but not least**, hold trust-building activities regularly.
