# 一 建议信

【题干 1】假设你是红星中学高二学生李华。你校交换生Jim打算在暑假期间游览一些中国历史文化名城，向你征询建议。请你用英文给他写一封邮件，内容包括: 

1. 推荐两个城市并说明理由;
2. 提出旅行注意事项。

注意: 1.词数100左右;

2.开头和结尾已给出，不计人总词数。

【@1】

## 范文

Dear Jim,  

I'm glad to hear that you're planning to visit some historical and cultural cities in China this summer. Here are two cities I highly recommend:  

First, Xi'an is a must-see. As one of China's ancient capitals, it's home to the world-famous Terracotta Warriors and the ancient City Wall. You can truly feel the profound history of the Tang and Qin dynasties there. Second, Beijing is another great choice. With landmarks like the Forbidden City, the Great Wall, and the Summer Palace, it offers a perfect blend of imperial grandeur and traditional culture.  

For your trip, I'd suggest booking tickets and hotels in advance, as summer is the peak tourist season. Also, remember to stay hydrated and wear sunscreen since it can be very hot. Finally, learning a few basic Mandarin phrases might be helpful for communication.  

Wish you a wonderful journey! Let me know if you need more details.  

Yours,  
Li Hua

# 二 回信

【题干 2】假设你是红星中学高三学生李华。你的英国好友 Jim 给你发邮件分享了他的新年计划，并询问你在新的一年里除学业之外有何打算。请你用英文给他回复，内容包括:

1. 你最想做的事;
2. 说明其原因。

注意: 1. 词数 100 左右;

2.开头和结尾已给出，不计入总词数。

【@2】

## 范文

Dear Jim,  

Thank you for sharing your New Year's resolutions with me – they sound really inspiring! As for me, apart from my studies, the one thing I most want to do this year is to learn to play the guitar.  

I've always been fascinated by music, and I think playing an instrument would be a perfect way to unwind after long hours of preparing for the college entrance exams. Besides, learning guitar demands patience and daily practice, which can help me build self‑discipline and perseverance. I also hope that by the end of the year, I can play a few simple songs and maybe even perform at our school's talent show or at my future university's welcome party, which would be a great memory!  

What about you? Do you have any new hobbies or goals besides your studies? I'd love to hear more.  

Yours sincerely,  
Li Hua
