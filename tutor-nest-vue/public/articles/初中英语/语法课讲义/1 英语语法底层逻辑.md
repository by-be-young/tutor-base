# 二、三条底层逻辑

## 规则一：一个简单句只能有一个谓语动词

英语里，一个简单句**有且只有一个**动词作谓语（即跟着主语变时态、变人称的那个）。

如果意思需要用到多个动词，多余的动词**不能直接用原形**，必须变身：

| 变身方式 | 什么时候用 |
| :---: | :--- |
| **to do** | 表目的、将来、具体某次动作 |
| **doing** | 作主语/宾语，表习惯或一般性行为 |
| **done** | 表被动或完成 |

**示例：**
- ❌ I want play basketball.
- ✅ I want **to play** basketball.

## 规则二：连词前后要"对等"

### 并列连词的对等

用 **and / but / or** 连接两个成分时，前后的**词性、结构必须一致**。

- 前面是名词，后面也得是名词
- 前面是 doing，后面也得是 doing
- 前面是 to do，后面也得是 to do

**示例：**
- ❌ I like **swimming** and **to run**.
- ✅ I like **swimming** and **running**.
- ✅ I like **to swim** and **to run**.

### 从句引导词的对等

**Because 和 so** 在英语里不能同时出现在同一个句子里。

**Although 和 but** 也一样，二者只能留一个。

**原因：** 因为 / 虽然已经是连词，后面再加 so / but 就只剩从句，没有主句了。

**示例：**
- ❌ Because I was tired, so I went to bed.
- ✅ Because I was tired, I went to bed.
- ✅ I was tired, so I went to bed.


# 三、推论公式：n 与 n-1 原则

在一个完整的句子结构中：

> **谓语动词总数 = 能增加谓语个数的连接词的个数 + 1**


**示例：**
- *I have a friend **who** speaks English.*
  - 动词：have、speaks → 2 个
  - 连词：who → 1 个
  - 2 = 1 + 1 ✅ 正确

- *I have a friend speaks English.*
  - 动词：have、speaks → 2 个
  - 连词：0 个
  - 2 ≠ 0 + 1 ❌ 错误！需要加连词或降级


# 四、回头改错（课前小测答案）

| 序号 | 原句 | 怎么改？ | 改后 |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | We use smartphones can communicate with friends. | can communicate 降级为 to communicate（表目的） | We use smartphones **to communicate** with friends. |
| 2 | Learn English helps us to read foreign articles. | Learn 作主语，改为 Learning | **Learning** English helps us to read foreign articles. |
| 3 | I like to eat bananas and drawing pictures. | and 两边统一，都用 doing 或都用 to do | I like **eating** bananas and **drawing** pictures. |
| 4 | Because I'm tired, so I want to sleep. | Because 和 so 删掉一个 | **Because** I'm tired, I want to sleep. / I'm tired, **so** I want to sleep. |


# 五、课堂练习

**判断下面句子对错，错的改正：**

1. There is a man wants to see you.
2. She enjoys reading books and to listen to music.
3. Although he was ill, but he still went to school.
4. I have a sister can sing very well.


## 答案

| 题号 | 判断 | 改后 |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | ❌ | There is a man **who wants** to see you. 或 There is a man **wanting** to see you. |
| 2 | ❌ | She enjoys **reading** books and **listening** to music. |
| 3 | ❌ | Although he was ill, he still went to school. 或 He was ill, **but** he still went to school. |
| 4 | ❌ | I have a sister **who can** sing very well. 或 I have a sister **able to** sing very well. |
